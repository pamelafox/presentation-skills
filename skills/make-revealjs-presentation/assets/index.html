The best slides in this deck style are crisp and declarative. Lead with the claim, then support it with three or four pieces of evidence rather than a wall of text.

  • Start with the decision, not the backstory.
  • Use a short subtitle only when the title needs context.

Show tradeoffs side by side

Option A

  • Best when you need speed or simplicity.
  • Lower upfront cost and less coordination.
  • Leaves more edge cases for later.

Option B

  • Better when safety, scale, or repeatability matter.
  • Takes more design work up front.
  • Produces cleaner long-term operating costs.
